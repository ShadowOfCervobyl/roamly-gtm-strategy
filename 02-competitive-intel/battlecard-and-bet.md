# Competitive Intelligence & Strategic Bet · Roamly Groups

## 1. Primary competitor

- **Competitor:** Airbnb Experiences
- **Core offering:** Host-led activities priced per person, the Airbnb Originals tier (exclusive, some with celebrities) and the new Services category (chefs, massages, photographers and similar), bookable on their own or alongside an Airbnb stay.
- **How they position themselves:**
  - Relaunched in May 2025 in more than 600 cities, alongside Airbnb Services.
  - Experiences exclusive to Airbnb, presented as part of the trip you have already booked.
  - Social features: guests can see who else is going, message the group and stay in touch afterwards.
- **Primary audience:** Airbnb's very large base of guests, many of whom already travel as groups because they share one stay.
- **Known pricing or packaging:**
  - Commission: historically 20% of the price, paid by the host. No official figure has been published since the 2025 relaunch; coverage only calls it "competitive with industry standards".
  - Pricing: per person, set by the host. Earlier versions let hosts offer private group bookings at a set minimum price; unconfirmed whether this continues after the relaunch.
  - Packaging: three tiers (Experiences, Originals, Services), booked on their own or with a stay. No subscription and no loyalty programme.
  - Group payments: split payments for stays only (up to 16 people, 72 hours each to pay). No evidence that they cover Experiences.
- **Why they're the biggest threat:**
  - They already have the group: friends who book a house together are one click from booking an activity together.
  - They already have the payments technology: split payments for stays have run since 2018.
  - If Airbnb extends split payments to Experiences, it owns the whole group trip: stay, activities and payment in one app.

##### Battlecard: Roamly Groups vs. Airbnb Experiences

###### 1. Know the Landscape

**Competitor Overview**
- **Who:** Airbnb's experiences business, next to its core business of stays
- **History:** relaunched May 2025 in 600+ cities, together with a new Services category
- **Serves:** Airbnb's guest base, including groups who already share one stay
- **Positioning:** experiences that are exclusive to Airbnb, plus Originals (some hosted by celebrities), presented as part of the trip you already booked
- **Why it matters:** the only competitor that already *has the group*

**Key Offerings**
| Offering | What it is | Relevance to Groups |
|---|---|---|
| Experiences | Activities run by hosts, priced per person | Direct overlap |
| Originals | Exclusive experiences, some with celebrities | Brand and buzz, low volume |
| Services | Chefs, massages, photographers and similar | Larger share of what a group spends |
| Split payments | **Stays only**: up to 16 people, 72 hours each to pay | Already working in Airbnb's stays |
| Group social features | See who's going, group messaging | Coordination, but no group decision |

**Pricing**
- **Guests:** no visible service fee
- **Stays:** single 15.5% fee paid by hosts (US rollout Oct–Dec 2025)
- **Experiences:** priced per person by the host. Commission historically 20% ⚠️
- **Split payments:** free
- **Packaging:** no subscription or loyalty programme. Book on its own or with a stay

---

###### 2. Understand the Battlefield

**Key Strengths**
1. **The group is already there:** the shared stay is one step from a shared activity
2. **Split-payment technology works,** live since 2018 for stays
3. **Scale and brand:** 600+ cities against Roamly's 40
4. **One trip in one app:** stay, activities and services together
5. **Guests already trust Airbnb** with payments, cancellations and support

**Key Weaknesses**
1. **Experiences are a side business.** The earlier version was scaled back
2. **Curation depth is unproven.** Originals are a showcase; the average experience is unknown
3. **No split payments for Experiences** (none found)
4. **No group decision tools.** Chat after booking isn't the same as agreeing before
5. **Only reaches Airbnb guests.** Hotel stays, family visits and road trips are out of reach
6. **High host fees (⚠️ about 20%)** are an opening to recruit hosts

---

###### 3. Win the Deal

**Why You Win (Roamly)**
| Advantage | Why it's defensible |
|---|---|
| Vetted local hosts | 12,000+ hosts vetted over years, 3× repeat booking. Scale can be bought; trust takes years |
| Built for the group decision | Shared plan, price per person and agreement before payment. Hard to add to a product where one person decides |
| Hosts ready for groups | Hosts who opt in, with group capacity and pricing. Supply is slow to copy |
| Experiences are the whole business | Every decision is about experiences. For Airbnb they're an add-on |
| Works however the group stays | Doesn't depend on where you sleep |

> ⚠️ **Split payment on its own is not defensible.** Airbnb can extend it quickly. Never lead with it. Lead with group agreement and host quality.

**Why They Win (Airbnb)**
- The stay is already booked on Airbnb, so it's the easiest path
- The city isn't one of Roamly's 40, so Roamly loses automatically
- The group wants one app for the whole trip
- Brand trust: "I know Airbnb"
- If Airbnb adds split payments to Experiences, Roamly loses its most visible difference

**Why You (one-liner)**
> "Airbnb gets you a place to stay. Roamly gets your whole group to agree on something worth doing, hosted by a vetted local, with everyone paying their own share."

###### 4. Handle the Hard Moments

**Quick Dismiss**
> "Airbnb is great for where you sleep. For what you do together, you want vetted hosts and a way for the whole group to agree and pay, without the organiser chasing anyone."

**Landmine Questions**
1. "When your group books an activity on Airbnb, who pays, and how do they get repaid?" → *no split payment for Experiences*
2. "How does your group decide what to book before anyone pays?" → *no group decision tools*
3. "Is your group staying at an Airbnb on every trip?" → *only reaches Airbnb guests*
4. "How do you know the host is good, beyond the stars?" → *unproven curation*
5. "What happens if someone drops out after booking?" → *group cancellation* (⚠️ ask only once Roamly's policy is ready)

**Objection Handling**
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We booked our stay on Airbnb." | "Keep it. Roamly works alongside any stay. The difference is vetted hosts, and the whole group agrees and pays in one place." |
| "Airbnb has more choice." | **Concede.** "In more cities, yes. In our 40, every host is vetted, and travellers come back 3× more often than the industry average." |
| "Airbnb already does split payments." | "For stays. For activities, the organiser still pays for everyone. And Roamly helps the group agree before anyone pays." |
| "I trust Airbnb." | "Fair. Our hosts are vetted before they join, and people come back three times as often because the experience delivered." |
| "One app is easier." | **Concede.** "Easier for booking, not for getting six people to agree and pay. Send one Roamly link and you're done." |
| "Your city isn't covered." | **Roamly loses.** Don't fight it. Log the request and use it to decide where to expand next. |

---

**Biggest risk:** Airbnb adds split payments and group planning to Experiences. Then Roamly's lead depends only on host quality and hosts ready for groups, so build those first.

## 4. Strategic bet

### Data
- **Who Roamly Groups is for:** The Roamly is for groups of people, who want to plan a group experience, and also share the payment for that. either friends, families, or anyone who is interested in a group experience bigger than a small party.
- **The one thing worth choosing it for:** Vetted providers, as far as the market shows 3x higher booking rate compared to industry data, and split payments.
- **The biggest threat to that position:** If the competition adopts split payments, or another competitor enters the market who provides similar services, but has more cities covered.

### Insight
There is a competition like Airbnb, Viator, and GetYourGuide so the market has other players. I believe when Roamly Groups will still focus on experience meaning rather less cities but more vetted providers, and improving the group's coordinating for the bookings, and split payments it has a pretty good chance to be successful.

### Belief
I now believe that Roamly Groups wins when it will be built for the group decision, not just payment because this is hard to bolt into a product that is designed for stays in the first part.

### Bet
- **I am betting that Roamly Groups wins by:** I am betting that Roamly Groups wins by building for the group decision, not just payments.
- **To make this bet, I am deprioritizing:** To make this bet, I am deprioritising incorporating new cities.
