# Positioning Framework & Statement · Roamly Groups

## 1. Positioning framework

- **For (target audience):** Groups of friends and families, bigger than a small party, who plan a shared experience together. The group decides, and one organiser brings the group to Roamly.
- **Who want / need:** "We want to find something we'll all enjoy, agree on it and the price, and book it without one person paying for everyone and chasing the money afterwards."
- **We offer:** Roamly Groups: one place for a group to plan, choose and book a local experience together. Everyone sees the plan and the price per person, agrees in one place, and pays their own share.
- **Because of (value proposition):** The group goes from "let's do something" to booked without the chasing. No one fronts the money, no one is left out of the decision, and everyone gets an experience run by a vetted local host.
- **Despite (barriers):** Roamly covers only 40 cities, against Airbnb Experiences' 600+. Many groups have already booked their stay on Airbnb, which is one click away from booking an activity there. Split payment on its own is not unique either: Airbnb already offers it for stays.
- **Better than (differentiation):** Better than Airbnb Experiences, Viator and GetYourGuide because Roamly Groups is built for the group decision, not just the payment. That decision layer is hard to add to a product built around one person booking. It is also backed by 12,000+ vetted hosts with a repeat booking rate three times the industry average, and that trust cannot be bought quickly.

## 2. Positioning statement

### First draft
For groups of friends and families planning a shared experience bigger than a small party, who want to agree on something they will all enjoy and book it without one person paying and chasing, Roamly Groups is a group booking feature that lets the whole group plan, choose and pay its own share in one place. Unlike Airbnb Experiences, Roamly Groups is built for the group decision and backed by 12,000+ vetted local hosts.

### Pressure test
- **Test I ran:**
  1. **Competitor swap:** replace "Roamly Groups" with "Airbnb Experiences" and check whether the statement is still true.
  2. **Customer language:** read the statement as the group would. Would they say these words themselves?
  3. **Barrier check:** does the statement hold up against the weakness I named (40 cities against 600+)?
- **What it revealed:**
  1. **"Pay its own share" fails the swap.** Airbnb already has split payments for stays and could extend them to Experiences. It is a feature anyone can copy, so it cannot be the reason to choose Roamly. What survives the swap is agreeing *before* anyone pays, and vetted hosts with proof (3× repeat booking).
  2. **"Built for the group decision" is internal language.** No traveller says it. The outcome they want is: "we all agree on something worth doing, and no one chases anyone for money".
  3. **The draft overpromised.** It claimed to be better than Airbnb everywhere, but Roamly loses outright in a city it does not cover. Limiting the claim to Roamly's 40 cities makes it defensible, and it matches my Module 2 decision to deprioritise new cities.
  4. **"Unlike Airbnb Experiences" was missing the contrast.** It said what Roamly is, not what Airbnb does differently. Naming the difference (one person books an activity next to a stay) makes it clear.

### Final statement
For friend and family groups planning a shared experience in one of Roamly's 40 cities, who want to agree on something they will all enjoy without one person paying for everyone and chasing the money, Roamly Groups is the group booking feature that takes the whole group from "let's do something" to booked: everyone sees the plan and their price, agrees in one place, and pays their own share. Unlike Airbnb Experiences, where one person books an activity alongside a stay, Roamly Groups is built around the group's decision and delivered by 12,000+ vetted local hosts whose travellers come back three times more often than the industry average.
